AI In Practice

What this looks like in practice.

Illustrative examples of the kind of workflow transformation we design and build — the shape of the work, not fabricated results.

Example — B2B services

Lead response & qualification

Before: Inbound leads sat in a shared inbox; reps manually researched and qualified each one before a first reply.

After: An AI agent researches the lead, scores intent, drafts a personalised first response and updates the CRM automatically — reps step in once a lead is qualified.

Example — Distribution & finance

Invoice processing

Before: Finance staff manually extracted line items from supplier invoices and re-keyed them into the accounting system.

After: Documents are parsed automatically, validated against purchase orders, routed for approval, and posted — with exceptions flagged for human review.

Example — Customer support

Ticket triage & response

Before: Every ticket needed a human to read, classify and search internal docs before replying.

After: Tickets are classified and answered from an approved knowledge base automatically, with complex or sensitive cases escalated straight to a human agent.
